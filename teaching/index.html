Activity Timeline
2025 Academic Lecture: The Impact of Urban Green Spaces on Public Health and Well-Being 10-23

Lecture Overview

Using the hyper-dense city of Hong Kong as a case study, Professor Yan Chen moved beyond the limitations of conventional research. Through a serial mediation model, she directly and indirectly examined the potential pathways linking the objective configuration of green space, subjective evaluations, use behavior, and self-reported health and well-being.

2025 Guangdong Graduate Academic Forum on Landscape Architecture 10-8

Forum Overview

This forum focuses on frontier theories, innovative practices, and emerging trends in urban environmental renewal and rural revitalization. It aims to explore the important role of landscape architecture in advancing integrated urban–rural development and the sustainable improvement of human settlements.
